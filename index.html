DV death review recommendations: who's acting on them?

Every state DV/family-violence death review body makes recommendations to stop the next death. This tracks what happened after — jurisdiction by jurisdiction, straight from each body's own reporting. First pass: headline numbers only, sourced from the most recent report that states them.

What the numbers say, by jurisdiction

NSW — DVDRT
40%
Partial
Of 122 recommendations made 2011–2019, 49 are implemented. 8% (10) have had no action at all. The Team paused issuing new recommendations after this report to focus on data analysis instead.
Source: DVDRT Report 2019–2021, Table 7.2
QLD — DFVDRAB
84%
Mostly actioned
Of 80 recommendations since 2016, 67 are implemented and 7 more are in progress. Only 1 was ever rejected (noted) by government.
Source: DFVDRAB Annual Report 2024–25, p.19
WA — Ombudsman
9/9
Claimed, not verified
All 9 recommendations from the 2022 investigation had "steps taken or proposed" per the 2023 follow-up — agency self-report, not confirmed completion. A separate 54 recommendations from 2015 are not yet reconciled here.
Source: 2023 implementation report

SA — State Coroner
No tally
Not publicly tracked
DV recommendations are issued inquest-by-inquest, buried inside general State Coroner annual reports. Checked the most recent report (2024–25): zero DV-specific recommendations that year. No cumulative count or implementation tracking found anywhere in the series.
Source: State Coroner annual reports, 17 years checked

These numbers aren't directly comparable to each other — different time windows, different self-reporting standards, different definitions of "implemented." Each tile stands on its own source, not on a shared scale. That unevenness is itself part of the story.

Jurisdictions with no comparable tracking

ACT
No mechanism
No domestic/family violence death review body exists. Confirmed via the Australian Human Rights Commission's 2016 call to expand DV death review to the jurisdictions that lack it.
TAS
No mechanism
Same gap as the ACT — no standing DV death review function. Nobody is systematically reviewing these deaths in either jurisdiction.
NT
Ad hoc only
Correction to an earlier assumption in this project: the NT is not a "nothing" jurisdiction like the ACT/TAS — it has no standing review body, but individual coronial inquests do make recommendations. The November 2024 inquest into the deaths of 4 Aboriginal women made 35 recommendations; the NT Government's July 2025 response claims 24 of 35 are "already in place" — a self-reported claim, not independently verified, and not something this tracker can confirm without the underlying evidence.
VIC
Discontinued
The Family Violence Reform Implementation Monitor tracked all 227 Royal Commission recommendations annually — the most thorough mechanism of any state — but the office closed 31 December 2022. Monitoring moved inside the Department of Premier & Cabinet and is no longer independent or published on the same annual cycle.
